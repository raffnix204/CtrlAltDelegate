# Local & Commerce SEO

Skill ID: `local-commerce-seo`
Library: `software-planning-lead-v5.8`
Version: `5.7.1`

## Purpose / Ownership
Own search-specific planning for local businesses and commerce catalogs where location/entity/product data changes page architecture and structured content.

## Activation & Negative Triggers
Activate for brick-and-mortar/service-area businesses, multi-location sites, product catalogs, marketplaces or commerce landing/category/product pages. Do not create fake location pages or load for generic SaaS with no local/commerce search need.

## Local Decision Model
Define real locations/service areas, canonical business identity, contact/hours/service facts, location page uniqueness, map/profile ownership and review handling. NAP/entity consistency is data integrity, not keyword decoration.

## Commerce Decision Model
Define category/product taxonomy, canonical/filter/facet strategy, product availability/price source of truth, structured-data ownership, variant handling, internal linking and content needed to answer purchase questions.

## Critical Invariants
No fabricated locations, reviews, inventory, prices or claims. Avoid doorway location pages and index bloat from filters/facets. Current Google/Bing/marketplace requirements are drift-sensitive and verified when consequential.

## Failure Modes / Sharp Edges
Duplicated city pages with swapped place names, inconsistent business identity, product schema diverging from visible price/availability, infinite parameter URLs, discontinued products with no lifecycle strategy and review markup without eligible first-party truth.

## Domain-Specific Verification
Check representative location/category/product route data, canonical/indexability, structured data, user task, trust/proof and source-of-truth synchronization.

## Companion Skills
`seo-strategy`, `structured-data-seo`, `technical-seo-engineering`, `database-design`, `ecommerce` specialists when available, `landing-conversion`.
