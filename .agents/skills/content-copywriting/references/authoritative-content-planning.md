# Authoritative Content Planning

Use when the planner is expected to produce implementation-ready public or product copy.

Research facts, audience, page/screen purpose and evidence before drafting. Generate structured content files rather than prose buried in a general plan. Each file records content status and source/claim constraints. After drafting, route `natural-content-editing`; for searchable pages also route SEO/SXO; for conversion surfaces route `landing-conversion`.

When `status: approved`, coding workers preserve the copy and CTA/factual meaning. Implementation may change markup/presentation but must not improvise new claims or rewrite approved content without scoped change control.
