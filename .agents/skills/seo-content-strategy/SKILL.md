# SEO Content Strategy & E-E-A-T

Skill ID: `seo-content-strategy`
Library: `software-planning-lead-v5.7.1`
Version: `5.7.1`

## Purpose / Ownership
Own content strategy for public searchable material. Optimize usefulness, trust, evidence and task completion first; search discoverability follows from satisfying real information needs.

## Activation & Negative Triggers
Activate for public page copy, editorial resources, product/service descriptions, documentation intended for discovery, or content refresh programs. Do not use to stuff keywords into UI microcopy or private/internal screens.

## Context To Inspect
Audience and intent map, product facts, source/evidence inventory, author/organization context, existing content, competitor/SERP evidence when available, freshness risk and conversion/user journey.

## Expert Decision Model
### 1. Apply Who / How / Why thinking without manufacturing signals
Where readers reasonably care, clarify who created the content, how claims/evidence were produced and why the page exists. Never invent credentials, first-hand experience or source authority.

### 2. Build briefs around user questions and evidence
Define page purpose, essential questions, required facts/examples/proof, objections, internal-link relationships, media/data needs and what must not be claimed.

### 3. Prefer topical sufficiency over word-count targets
Use as much content as needed to satisfy the task with clarity. Page length, keyword density and arbitrary link counts are not success metrics.

### 4. Make important claims easy to understand and source
Clear headings, direct answers, self-contained explanations, tables/lists when useful and explicit attribution improve both human comprehension and machine extraction. Treat platform-specific GEO tactics as drift-sensitive hypotheses unless supported by current first-party guidance.

## Critical Invariants
- Claims remain evidence-backed and product-true.
- Content is written for users, not search-engine manipulation.
- AI-assisted drafting does not justify scaled low-value duplication.
- Search/GEO heuristics never override factual accuracy or natural language.

## Failure Modes / Sharp Edges
Thin templated location/service pages, copied competitor structure with no value, fake expertise, unsupported statistics, forced keyword repetition, freshness churn without substantive update and generic LLM prose that hides concrete meaning.

## Domain-Specific Verification
For each key page verify intent fit, factual/source anchors, unique value, heading/information structure, internal-link role, metadata brief and naturalness/voice review. Use `natural-content-editing` after drafting and `search-experience-optimization` when SERP/page-type alignment is material.
