# Research, Feasibility and Drift — V5.9.2

Research is recurring (`R0`..`R4`) and claim-driven. A feasibility-critical claim cannot be left unproven behind broad dependent implementation. Use the smallest decisive probe/spike; record evidence/freshness/falsification method. Factual claims may receive a fresh skeptic. Judgment decisions use criteria/ADR/authority rather than agent voting. Execution evidence that refutes a strategic claim triggers scoped replan/rebrief.
